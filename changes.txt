### Task: Refactor and Polish About Page UI/UX & Performance

Refactor the About page component (`/about`) to elevate visual hierarchy, contrast, micro-interactions, and rendering performance. The tech stack is Next.js 15, React 19, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion.

---

### 1. Typography & Contrast Refinements
- **Stat Labels & Subtext:** Increase contrast for secondary text (e.g., "Full-Stack MERN & PWAs", "Web & AI Product Engineering"). Change dark grey text classes from `text-zinc-600` / `text-slate-600` to a clearer `text-slate-400` or `text-zinc-300` against the dark background.
- **Large Stat Numbers:** Adjust letter spacing on the main counter numbers (`12+`, `3+`, `100%`, `25k+`) with `tracking-tight` or `tracking-tighter` to give them a modern, high-density look.
- **Section Headers:** Ensure section labels (e.g., `ABOUT ME`, `THE JOURNEY`, `THE ARSENAL`) have consistent letter-spacing (`tracking-widest`), uppercase styling, and subtle opacity (`text-blue-400/80` or `text-zinc-400`).

---

### 2. Spacing, Layout & Micro-Visuals
- **Stat Cards Grid:** 
  - Increase vertical padding inside the stat cards container (`py-8` or `py-10`).
  - Add a subtle border divider between columns (`border-zinc-800/60` or `border-blue-900/30`).
  - Add a soft radial gradient glow behind the stats grid using a pseudo-element or Tailwind:
    `bg-[radial-gradient(ellipse_at_center,_var(--tw-gradient-stops))] from-blue-600/10 via-transparent to-transparent`.
- **Wireframe Icons & Index Tags:** 
  - Scale up top wireframe icons by ~15-20% and ensure a stroke width of `1.5px` or `2px`.
  - Align index numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`) neatly alongside the icons or above the main stat headers.

---

### 3. Motion & Micro-Interactions (Framer Motion)
- **Hover Micro-interactions:** Add hardware-accelerated hover effects to stat cards:
  - Slight upward translation: `whileHover={{ y: -3 }}`
  - Subtle background or border glow transition: `hover:border-blue-500/30 hover:bg-blue-950/20`
  - Ensure `transition={{ duration: 0.2, ease: "easeOut" }}`.
- **Scroll-Triggered Counter Animation:**
  - Animate numbers when entering the viewport using Framer Motion's `useInView` and `useSpring` (or `animate()`) so numbers count up smoothly upon reveal.

---

### 4. Technical Performance & Optimization
- **Hardware Acceleration:** Apply `will-change-transform` or `transform-gpu` to animated cards to offload rendering to the GPU.
